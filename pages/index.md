This Snow Camp Museum Studies and Public History research project provides insight into archival best practices, preservation, and historical context through glimpses into the lives of Quakers living in and around Snow Camp, North Carolina in the late 1800s/early 1900s. Within this project, there are key questions being asked: Why were these items important to people? Do they remain important and how can they be used today?
