This Snow Camp Museum Studies and Public History research project provides insight into archival practices, preservation, and * through glimpses into the lives of Quaker's living in and around Snow Camp, North Carolina in the late 1800s/early 1900s. Within this project, there is a key question being asked: Why were these items important to people? Do they remain important and how can they be used today?
